Your turn

Jam room

Pick a backing, press Start, and play whatever comes. Every note is coloured by what it is doing: landing (a chord tone, or Sa and Pa over the drone), passing through the scale, or outside it.

  1. Before a phrase, pick where it will land. Wander on the way.
  2. Keep phrases short, and breathe between them.
  3. Take an idea you just played and change one thing: its rhythm, its starting note, its direction.
  4. Over chords, land on the new chord's notes when it arrives.
